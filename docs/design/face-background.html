The background is the same hue as the eyes, dropped to about 8% lightness. The whole panel bathes in the mood. Warmest and most alive, and the mood reads instantly from across the room even if you can't make out the eye shape.

I'd take A. You said the face has colours and moods — in B and C the mood only lives in two small bars, where in A the whole panel carries it. All three sit between 6% and 10% lightness, so none of them will light up a dark room at 2am.
