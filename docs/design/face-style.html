Which eyes should it have?

All three wake the same way — shut, then opening. Watch for a few seconds and they'll blink on their own. Click the one that feels right.

A — Pills

Tall rounded bars. The most readable across a room, and the easiest to bend into moods: squash them to squint, round the tops to smile.

B — Round, with lids

Circles with a real eyelid dropping over them. The most creaturely of the three — it reads as something alive rather than a device.

C — Strokes

Two thin arcs, barely there. Coolest and most restrained, but it has the least range — hard to look genuinely hurt with only a line.

And the moods

Shown in style A. Colour sets the temperature, but the eye shape does most of the work — colour alone can't tell sad from sleepy.
